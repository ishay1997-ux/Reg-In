# Miss-ledger — the manager's calibration record (REG-IN)

**Append the moment a miss surfaces — not at session end.** This file is the
validation instrument of the work-manager skill: without it the manager
accumulates confidence, not skill (710's design, adopted 01/08/2026; there it
replaced a dropped fresh-agent test as the only evidence that the skill works).

**What counts as an entry:** an approval of yours that didn't hold · a finding
that surfaced after you declared a round closed · a question you asked Ishay that
the repo/browser could have answered · an escalation misclassified in either
direction (asked him what you owned; decided what needed his field knowledge).
**What doesn't:** a builder's own bug you never claimed to have checked · generic
hindsight ("could have been faster").

Format — one row per miss, newest first:

| תאריך | מה פספסתי | מה זה לימד (שורת-הכלל שנולדה, אם נולדה) |
